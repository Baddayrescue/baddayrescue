Thanks for testing Bad Day Rescue

Free beta · October 2026

Bad Day Rescue is a free emergency guide that works with no internet. Your testing helps make sure it works when someone really needs it. Plan on about 20 minutes.

1. Install it on your phone

iPhone: open baddayrescue.com in Safari. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: open baddayrescue.com in Chrome. Tap the three-dot menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

2. Test it with no internet

  1. Open the app from your home screen once while you have service, then close it.
  2. Turn on airplane mode, with Wi-Fi off too.
  3. Open the app again from your home screen. It should load and work.
  4. Try every tab: Frequencies, Shelters, Hospitals, Emergency vets, Gear and How to use.
  5. Turn airplane mode back off.

3. Check your own area

  1. Tap your FEMA region. Is your local AM emergency station listed?
  2. In Hospitals, search your town or ZIP code. Is the nearest ER there, with the right phone number?
  3. In Emergency vets, search your city or state. Do you know of a 24-hour vet that's missing or closed?
  4. Star a frequency, close the app, and open it again. Is your star still there?

4. Tell us what you found

Email feedbackbaddayrescue.unveiling737@passinbox.com with:

  • Your phone (for example, iPhone 15 or Samsung Galaxy S24)
  • Did it work in airplane mode? Yes or no
  • Anything wrong: the tab, the name, and what it should say
  • Anything confusing, or anything you wish it had

Please don't call hospitals or vets just to test numbers unless you've been asked to. A quick web search of the number is enough.
