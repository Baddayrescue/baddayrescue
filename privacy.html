Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Bad Day Rescue does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no tracking and no ads that follow you.

What stays on your device

When you star a frequency, that list is saved only in your own browser on your own device. We never see it, and it is erased if you clear your browser's data.

To work without internet, the app saves a copy of itself on your device the first time you open it. That copy contains only the app and its built-in lists.

What we don't do

  • We don't use analytics, cookies or tracking tools.
  • We don't ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • We don't sell or share data, because we don't collect any.

Our website host

Like every website, the company that hosts baddayrescue.com may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the date of each visit, for security and to keep the site running. We do not use those logs to identify or track anyone.

Links to other websites

Some buttons open other websites, such as the Red Cross shelter map, government sites, or stores on our Gear page. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Some Gear links are affiliate links, which means the store may know you came from Bad Day Rescue.

Children

Bad Day Rescue is a general-audience emergency guide. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
